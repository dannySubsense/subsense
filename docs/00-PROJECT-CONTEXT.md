# Subsense.art Rebuild — Project Context Packet

**Status:** Canonical context for project initialization  
**Project:** Rebuild and reimagine `subsense.art`  
**Owner and creative director:** Danny Clarke  
**Initial working environment:** Home-lab server, with Codex working locally and GitHub serving as the durable source and review surface  
**Purpose of this document:** Preserve the intellectual, artistic, technical, and product context that led to the rebuild so that the project is never flattened into “make an artist portfolio website.”

---

## 1. How to use this document

This document is the long-form memory of the project. It exists because the ideas behind Subsense emerged across several conversations and adjacent projects. A short build prompt would preserve the assignment while losing the reasoning, tensions, references, and future seams that make the project distinctive.

This is not a feature specification and should not be treated as a list of everything that must be built now. It contains three kinds of material:

1. **Active project truth** — decisions and principles that should shape the immediate rebuild.
2. **Supporting context** — adjacent ideas that explain the project but should not become immediate features.
3. **Future possibility** — directions the architecture should not foreclose, but which are explicitly outside the first release.

The build agent should read this document before proposing information architecture, visual direction, schemas, frameworks, or implementation plans. It should then work from the narrower scope documents and decision log created during the build.

The governing rule is:

> Preserve the whole conceptual field, but activate only the portion needed for the current build.

This protects both sides of the project. The richness is not forgotten, and the first website is not buried beneath the weight of every connected system.

## 2. The clearest statement of the project

Subsense is Danny Clarke’s evolving studio, archive, and publication: a public place for art, writing, research, references, experiments, and the connections among them.

The immediate project is to rebuild `subsense.art` from the materials of the former website, but not to reproduce the former website. The old site is a source corpus. It holds evidence of the work, language, chronology, and earlier self-understanding. The new site must preserve what remains true, reframe what has developed, archive what no longer belongs in the public presentation, and provide a structure that can expand without another foundational redesign.

The website should become a public window into how Danny sees and thinks—not merely a grid of completed artwork.

That means the site may eventually hold:

- Artwork and projects
- Personal and critical writing
- Research and lines of inquiry
- Visual and cultural references
- Curated collections or “constellations”
- Experiments and works in progress
- Relationships among art, sound, material, place, architecture, technology, fabrication, AI, and learning
- Selected tools, systems, and processes where they illuminate the practice rather than overwhelm it

The first version does not need to manifest all of these equally. It needs to establish a truthful center and an expandable structure.

## 3. Why the rebuild is happening now

The former Subsense site was taken down. Its contents survive in screenshots, images, copy, project information, and other saved materials. At the same time, several previously separate lines of thought have begun converging:

- Rebuilding the artist website from saved material
- Creating better-looking and more personally authored pages than generic AI-generated interfaces
- Collecting visual references, screenshots, bookmarks, social saves, videos, and browsing history
- Understanding why certain images, sites, objects, layouts, and cultural artifacts attract Danny’s attention
- Turning tacit preference into explainable judgment
- Using those judgments to steer Claude Code, Codex, and other implementation agents
- Building learning paths and knowledge graphs rather than flat resource collections
- Preserving provenance and connective tissue instead of accumulating decontextualized links
- Exploring how a personal archive can become a public cultural world

Subsense is the first real surface where these ideas can meet without requiring the complete machinery behind them to exist first.

This is why the rebuild should begin now rather than waiting for Recall, ICM workflows, a generalized taste product, or a mature learning platform. A real website with real content creates the most useful pressure. It reveals which structures, tools, and ideas are necessary and which are merely attractive abstractions.

## 4. Danny Clarke: the practice the site must actually represent

Danny has described himself as an interdisciplinary artist-researcher. That phrase remains useful, but the new website must demonstrate what it means rather than leave it as a generalized label.

The practice has involved:

- Sensory experience
- Multimedia narrative
- Immersive and expanded listening
- Sonic knowing
- Experimental storytelling
- Recording and archiving
- Personal and collective counter-memory
- Land, site, space, and place
- The relationship between bodily experience and material form
- Computational design and fabrication
- The translation of intangible phenomena into physical artifacts

One important body of work translates recorded audio frequencies into ceramic 3D-printed vessels. This is not merely a technical novelty. It joins sound, bodily memory, collective activity, time, land, site, space, computation, and material fabrication. That conjunction is emblematic of the larger practice: experiences and systems that normally remain invisible are made sensible, spatial, material, or narratable.

The new Subsense should therefore resist presenting the work as isolated objects. Where appropriate, a project page should make room for:

- The originating question or condition
- Site and cultural context
- Source recordings or other evidence
- Process and transformation
- Materials and fabrication
- Images and finished artifacts
- Text, captions, and reflections
- Relationships to other work

The site should not become an exhaustive technical archive on every page. But it should make it possible to see that the objects arise from inquiry, listening, translation, memory, and making.

Subsense should also make room for Danny’s broader life as a builder, technologist, architectural thinker, and systems composer without collapsing the artist identity into a professional résumé. Architecture, computational design, fabrication, software, AI, and organizational systems are not unrelated hobbies. They reflect recurring attention to how intent becomes structure and how structure becomes experience.

## 5. The central idea: an authored world

The deepest aspiration is not simply “a tasteful site.” It is an authored world.

An authored world has:

- A recognizable point of view
- Accumulated attention
- Cultural and material specificity
- Relationships that are not obvious until the author places things together
- A sense of time, development, and habitation
- Room for both formal work and exploratory wandering
- Exclusions as deliberate as inclusions
- A person behind the arrangement

This matters because generic AI-generated sites can approximate surface style—a font, palette, grid, hero treatment, or fashionable animation—without possessing accumulated culture. They can reproduce signs of taste without the judgments that produced them.

Subsense should not ask an agent to invent Danny’s world. It should make Danny’s existing world legible and navigable, then give agents enough explicit context to help compose within it.

The website is therefore both:

1. A public expression of the world.
2. A forcing function for externalizing the judgment that organizes it.

## 6. The references that clarified the project

The following precedents are not visual templates. Each revealed a different capability the Subsense system may need.

### 6.1 The Selvedge Yard: depth, texture, and associative wandering

The Selvedge Yard is important less because of its old WordPress theme than because of the mind visible through it. It is a historical and cultural archive built from a strong personal eye: motorcycles, racing, clothing, denim, leather, music, film, craft, photography, makers, postwar Americana, rebellion, and subculture.

Its characteristic movement is:

```text
Object or person
→ history
→ material
→ image
→ cultural meaning
→ adjacent people and scenes
→ another rabbit hole
```

A motorcycle jacket is not only apparel. It opens into functional design, manufacturing, film, youth identity, material durability, archetype, and rebellion. The object is an entry point into a cultural network.

The Selvedge Yard contributes several essential principles:

- The real interface can be the curator’s associative intelligence.
- Long-form context and imperfect accumulation can create texture.
- A site can reward wandering without forcing an immediate takeaway.
- Tags and relationships can expose unexpected cultural trails.
- Archives feel inhabited when time and accumulation remain visible.
- Organization should not sterilize the material.
- Connective tissue is often more valuable than any isolated bookmark.

Its warning for Subsense is equally important:

> Cleaning and systematizing the corpus too aggressively could remove the texture that makes it alive.

Subsense should borrow The Selvedge Yard’s permission to be dense, personal, historical, and exploratory—not its exact aesthetic or subject matter.

### 6.2 Anam-Cara and the Free Degree Builder: packaging, pathways, and taste as learnable

Anam-Cara’s public work and Free Degree Builder clarified a different layer: how curation becomes approachable, sequenced, and useful.

The Free Degree Builder organizes a finite set of free university courses into identity-driven 12-week pathways. Its value is not the number of courses. It is the editorial logic that gives each course a role in a progression.

The “Develop Taste” sequence joins:

```text
Look slowly
→ interpret structurally
→ compress deliberately
→ exercise judgment
```

Its underlying proposition is that taste can be developed through exposure, attention, history, vocabulary, comparison, interpretation, compression, selection, and exclusion.

An especially useful distinction emerged:

**Preference** says, “I like this.” Preference is personal and valid, but another person or an agent cannot reliably act on it.

**Taste** says, “I favor this because of these relationships.” Taste can be observed, explained, challenged, transferred, tested, and used to guide a new decision.

The transformation relevant to Subsense is:

```text
Preference
→ observation
→ explanation
→ principle
→ constraint
→ application
→ evaluation
```

Anam-Cara also shows the value of:

- Finite, opinionated collections
- Cross-disciplinary learning
- Editorial framing
- Naming the job each resource performs
- Turning intimidating institutional material into an accessible invitation
- Creating small public tools that demonstrate the quality of the underlying curation

But the model is incomplete for Danny’s purposes. It largely stops before deliberate practice, critique, validation, and production. It can guide someone toward material without proving that the learner can exercise or transfer the resulting judgment.

Subsense should take the pathway logic and editorial accessibility while preserving more depth, ambiguity, practice, and evaluation.

### 6.3 Index Zero: visible pathways through capability

Index Zero belongs to a separate product line: a learning and capability coach, potentially using seven-day programs, research extensions, visible progress, and a spatial or knowledge-graph representation of development.

It connects to Subsense because both ask how a body of material becomes a navigable path rather than a static collection. A Subsense constellation could eventually become a learning path; an Index Zero program could draw on a curated corpus. But they are not the same product.

The boundary is firm:

- **Subsense** is the public studio, archive, publication, and cultural atlas.
- **Index Zero** is a structured learning and capability-development product.

Subsense may contain paths through its archive. It should not absorb Index Zero’s coaching, progress, program, or product requirements during the initial rebuild.

### 6.4 Matt Pocock’s learning graph: definitions that lead to other definitions

The earlier learning-graph discussion introduced the idea that understanding is not a linear sequence of pages. A concept can lead to prerequisite concepts, adjacent distinctions, examples, counterexamples, and applications.

This matters to Subsense because a constellation is not merely a gallery or tag page. It may become a path through relationships. The graph should serve comprehension and discovery, however; a visible network visualization is not automatically the best interface. The user experience should reveal meaningful connections without turning every relationship into eye-candy nodes and lines.

### 6.5 Typology, micrographics, and visual-reference accounts

Instagram posts involving typologies, micrographics, designed collections, and visual classification helped surface a more applied need: Danny wants better tools for steering the visual design of personal writing, artist pages, applications, and other interfaces.

The attraction is not a single house style. It includes:

- The intelligence of grouping
- Comparison across variants
- Small systems of type, notation, rails, captions, and indexing
- The way a page can make research visually legible
- The combination of editorial voice and systematic structure

These references should enter the project as evidence to examine, not as decoration to imitate.

## 7. From bookmark to constellation

Conventional bookmark systems treat the saved URL as the basic unit. That is too small and too context-poor for this project.

A bookmark says, “this was saved.”

A reference record says, “this object, page, image, passage, building, garment, or interface contains something worth examining.”

A constellation says, “these people, objects, images, places, materials, periods, and ideas belong together for a reason that the curator can articulate.”

The constellation is the more useful public and intellectual unit.

A constellation may contain:

- A central subject, question, or tension
- Original sources and provenance
- Images, objects, texts, audio, and video
- People, places, periods, materials, disciplines, and events
- Danny’s notes and interpretation
- Relationships to adjacent constellations
- A claim about what the grouping reveals
- Transferable principles, where appropriate
- Open questions and unresolved contradictions

This is more meaningful than tagging six independent links `architecture`, `fashion`, `sound`, or `vintage`. Tags classify. A constellation preserves the connective explanation.

For Subsense, constellations are the expandable seam between a conventional artist site and the larger cultural atlas. The first release may publish only one. Its existence in the information architecture signals the direction without requiring a mature atlas platform.

A strong candidate for the first constellation is:

**On Taste, Curation, and Authored Worlds**  
Anam-Cara → The Selvedge Yard → preference versus taste → why an archive becomes more than a collection → how curated judgment can influence what one makes.

This candidate has special value because it documents the thinking that helped precipitate the rebuild itself.

## 8. The larger system surrounding Subsense

Several connected systems have been discussed. Their responsibilities must remain distinct even if they eventually share schemas, files, or infrastructure.

### 8.1 Recall / Bookmark Retrieval

Recall is the intake and memory layer.

Its basic responsibility is:

```text
Capture
→ normalize
→ preserve provenance
→ extract
→ store
→ relate
→ retrieve
→ resurface
```

Potential sources include Instagram saves and shares, YouTube, websites, browser history, screenshots, exports, documents, and other public or personal material.

Recall must preserve more than the link. Depending on source and permissions, it should preserve:

- The original creator and source
- When and how the item was encountered
- The underlying media or a durable representation
- Captions, transcripts, or extracted text
- Relevant images or representative frames
- Source truth separately from machine interpretation
- Danny’s “personal pull”: what attracted attention
- Relationships and later uses

The preferred acquisition posture is share-first forward capture, with connectors or exports for backfill. Screenshots and media should retain provenance. Agent-owned social accounts may be an optional laboratory, not the foundation.

Recall answers:

> What did I encounter, what was it, where did it come from, and how can I find it again?

It does not by itself answer:

> Why does this matter to my taste, and how should it change what I make?

### 8.2 Taste or visual intelligence

The taste-intelligence layer interprets selected material.

It may help identify:

- What precisely caught Danny’s attention
- Typography, hierarchy, grid, scale, density, rhythm, image treatment, notation, micrographics, motion, or material qualities
- Desired emotional or cognitive effects
- Similar and contrasting examples
- Repeated preferences
- Negative preferences and clichés to avoid
- Provisional principles
- Transfer boundaries: where a principle applies and where it does not
- Evidence status: raw preference, observed pattern, tested hypothesis, validated personal rule, project-specific rule, or rejected rule

This layer answers:

> What do I respond to, why might it work, and what does that imply?

The system should begin with reference-conditioned retrieval and explicit human interpretation—not with training a model to claim it has learned Danny’s taste.

### 8.3 ICM / Interpretable Context Methodology

ICM is a method for assembling selected context into a workspace an agent can understand and walk. `icm-architect` is a potential generator or projector of those workspaces.

The durable distinction is:

> Recall finds the shelf; ICM builds the room.

Or more technically:

- Recall owns memory, durable capture, and retrieval.
- ICM owns context delivery for a bounded task.

For this rebuild, an ICM-style project room might contain:

- Old-site screenshots
- Recovered copy
- Original artwork and media
- Project titles, dates, collaborators, technologies, exhibitions, and descriptions
- Selected visual references
- Positive and negative taste notes
- Page requirements
- Current decisions
- Open questions
- Generated outputs and reviews

The underlying pattern is “database underneath, folders above”: durable canonical material can live in an appropriate store, while agents receive a human-readable, selectively assembled workspace for the current task.

ICM is supporting infrastructure. It does not need to be a visible feature of Subsense.

### 8.4 AI design steering

Design steering is the applied layer that converts selected references and articulated judgment into project direction.

Instead of prompting:

> Make me a tasteful artist website.

Danny should eventually be able to assemble a project-specific constellation such as:

- The archival density and wandering of The Selvedge Yard
- The typographic confidence and finite editorial choices of the Free Degree Builder
- A particular magazine’s image rhythm
- A restrained material palette grounded in the artwork
- Micrographic notation and captions from selected references
- A negative constraint against generic SaaS cards
- Mobile readability and accessibility requirements
- The actual content hierarchy of the Subsense corpus

The system could translate this into:

- A creative brief
- Design principles
- Page-specific direction
- Implementation parameters
- Explicit “avoid” rules
- Acceptance criteria
- A rendered comparison and critique loop

Agents should not manufacture aesthetic authority. They help extract, articulate, implement, compare, and revise. Danny retains authorship and judgment.

### 8.5 Subsense

Subsense is where selected outputs become public expression.

It consumes only what has earned a place. It is not the raw bookmark inbox, the entire research database, the ICM workspace, or the agent control plane.

Subsense answers:

> What am I prepared to place into the world, in this relationship, under my name?

## 9. The full conceptual pipeline

The larger system implied by these conversations is:

```text
CAPTURE
Save a post, screenshot, website, building, spread, object, passage,
recording, interface, or other reference.

RESOLVE
Identify the original creator, project, date, source, discipline,
and relevant context.

EXTRACT
Identify the exact detail, relationship, or quality that attracted attention.

INTERPRET
Explain why the detail may be effective and what it does.

COMPARE
Place it beside similar and contrasting examples.

DISTILL
Turn repeated observations into a provisional principle.

TEST
Apply the principle in a bounded creative decision.

EVALUATE
Compare the result with the intended effect and source reasoning.

PROMOTE
Add a successful principle to a personal or project-specific grammar.

INSTRUCT
Translate selected principles into direction for an implementation agent.

VERIFY
Review the rendered work, provenance, and behavior—not merely the code.

PUBLISH
Place the resolved work, writing, or constellation into Subsense.
```

The immediate rebuild begins near the end of this pipeline with an existing body of work. It should not attempt to automate the entire pipeline before publishing the site.

The composer:

- Selects and rejects material
- Establishes relationships
- Sets constraints

- Decides which ambiguities should remain
- Chooses where agents may exercise judgment

Claude Code, Codex, and related agents may:

- Inventory and extract
- Propose structures
- Identify inconsistencies and missing data

- Create variants
- Apply deterministic checks
- Render pages
- Compare implementations with explicit criteria
- Maintain documentation and provenance

The system should externalize enough of Danny’s intent that agents can work coherently without pretending to replace his judgment.

```text
Human intent and selection
→ explicit project context
→ bounded agent work
→ rendered evidence
→ human judgment

```

This is why persistent project documents and decision logs matter more than a single large prompt.

## 11. Initial information architecture

The provisional public structure is:

### Index

An editorial entry point rather than a conventional marketing homepage. It should orient a first-time visitor while also making the site feel alive to a returning visitor.

Possible ingredients:

- Selected work
- Recent or foundational writing
- One active line of inquiry
- One constellation
- A concise orientation to Danny’s practice
- Signals of relationships across the site

The Index should not attempt to summarize everything. It should establish voice, scale, and paths.

### Work

The body of artwork and projects reconstructed from the old site and source materials.

Projects may vary in available evidence. The content model should support a minimal project record without forcing empty fields, while allowing richer projects to include audio, process, site, materials, research, fabrication, collaborators, exhibitions, and related writing.

### Writing

Essays, notes, observations, project texts, and research. Writing is a first-class form, not supporting copy attached to the art.

The system may eventually distinguish essays, field notes, annotations, and shorter fragments, but it should not create unnecessary taxonomic complexity before the corpus is inventoried.

### Constellations

Curated, interpreted groupings that connect references, work, writing, people, materials, periods, places, and questions.

This is the key expansion seam. It should begin modestly and become richer only through actual use.

### About

A clear statement of practice, relevant biography, and contact information. It should connect the artist-researcher identity to the work without becoming a corporate profile.

This structure is provisional until the old corpus is inventoried. The corpus—not a generic portfolio convention—should determine final navigation and hierarchy.

## 12. Desired visual and experiential character

The site should feel:

- Authored
- Editorial
- Asymmetric but disciplined
- Culturally and materially grounded
- Capable of quietness and density
- Typographically confident
- Attentive to image sequence and scale
- Rewarding to wander
- Precise without becoming sterile
- Contemporary without performing trendiness
- Built over time rather than generated in one pass

Potential interface vocabulary includes:

- Strong typographic hierarchy
- Deliberate contrasts in scale
- Editorial indexes
- Rails, marginalia, captions, and cross-references
- Dense metadata where it contributes meaning
- Generous whitespace where it creates attention
- Image sequences rather than interchangeable thumbnails
- Restrained, meaningful motion
- Visible relationships among projects, writing, and constellations
- Mobile-readable type and navigation

The design should respond to the actual artwork. It must not impose a strong editorial gesture that competes with or homogenizes materially different projects.

### Explicit negative direction

Avoid:

- Generic SaaS landing-page composition
- A grid of identical rounded cards
- Decorative gradients used as a substitute for identity
- Icon clutter
- A fashionable hero section disconnected from the content
- A symmetrical thumbnail portfolio as the entire organizing system
- A visually impressive knowledge graph with no meaningful navigational role
- Generic “creative technologist” language
- Style prompts that substitute for position, audience, content, and purpose
- Over-smoothing the archive until it loses history and texture
- Invented biography, exhibitions, provenance, meaning, or project facts
- Explaining future systems more prominently than presenting existing work

The standard is not “does this look designed?” It is:

> Do the relationships among purpose, audience, content, visual language, interaction, and expressive objective feel intentional?

## 13. Content recovery before design

The first working phase is archaeological.

The immediate question is:

> What did the old site contain?

It is not yet:

> What should the new site ultimately become?

The working corpus should initially include:

```text
00-old-site-screenshots/
01-artwork-and-media/
02-old-copy/
03-project-information/
04-visual-references/
05-current-writing/
06-open-questions/
```

The first inventory should be non-destructive. Do not prematurely rename, relocate, deduplicate, rewrite, or normalize everything. Record what exists, identify apparent relationships, and flag uncertainty.

Each piece of old-site material should eventually be classified:

### Preserve

Still accurately represents Danny and should return substantially intact.

### Transform

Contains valuable work or history but needs new framing, editing, sequencing, metadata, media treatment, or contextualization.

### Retire

Should remain in the private archive but not return to the public site.

Retirement is not deletion. The project must distinguish archival preservation from public inclusion.

Unknown facts should remain explicit unknowns. Agents must not fill gaps with plausible invention.

## 14. Provenance, evidence, and interpretation

Provenance is part of the creative and technical integrity of Subsense.

The system should distinguish:

- Source facts
- Original media
- Recovered old-site language
- Danny’s current interpretation
- Agent-generated summaries or proposals
- Publicly approved copy

This separation matters because the new site will reinterpret old material. It should be possible to know whether a sentence came from the original site, was newly written by Danny, or was proposed by an agent and approved later.

For cultural references, retain original creator and source wherever possible. Curators and discovery sources should not replace the authorship of the underlying work.

For generated or transformed media, preserve the relationship to the source asset and document material changes.

The project should use deterministic tools for facts that can be checked—file inventories, dimensions, links, schema validity, builds, accessibility tests, and regressions. Agents may assist with interpretation, but should not manufacture evidence or bypass verification.

## 16. Build methodology: tracer bullets

The project should proceed through complete, narrow paths rather than broad layers of unfinished infrastructure.

The first tracer bullet is:

```text
Recovered source material
→ inventoried project
→ editorial decision
→ structured content
→ designed page
→ local implementation
→ rendered review
→ revision
```

This proves that the process can move from historical evidence to a finished public experience.

A second tracer bullet may be:

```text
Captured references
→ provenance
→ interpretation
→ relationship
→ constellation
→ published page
```

Only after these paths work should the project generalize capture tools, schemas, generators, visual graphs, or multi-user product behavior.

The point of a tracer bullet is not to create a disposable prototype. It is to send one thin, production-minded line through the system so unknowns become visible early.

## 18. Agent operating doctrine

Agents working on Subsense should follow these rules:

1. **Read before acting.** Inspect the project context, active scope, decision log, and relevant source material.
2. **Do not invent evidence.** Unknown titles, dates, materials, credits, exhibitions, biographies, and meanings remain unknown until resolved.
3. **Separate source from interpretation.** Preserve original copy and media even when proposing revisions.
4. **Keep the current task bounded.** Do not turn every useful adjacent idea into a feature.
5. **Show the work visually.** For design decisions, render pages at relevant viewport sizes. Code quality alone is not acceptance.
6. **Preserve reversibility.** Use version control and small, intelligible commits.
7. **Document consequential decisions.** Do not leave project intent trapped in a chat transcript.
8. **Use references analytically.** Identify the relevant relationship or principle; do not imitate a reference wholesale.
9. **Respect negative direction.** “Avoid” rules are part of the design system.
10. **Escalate judgment.** When a decision changes authorship, public meaning, major information architecture, or project representation, present evidence and ask Danny to decide.
11. **Prefer the simplest architecture that preserves meaning.** Future possibility is not permission for premature infrastructure.
12. **Verify the experience.** Test links, media, responsive behavior, accessibility, performance, and content integrity.

- Identify missing or ambiguous material.

### Next

Define the first public release from evidence.

- Classify material as Preserve, Transform, or Retire.
- Confirm the site’s positioning and navigation.
- Select representative content for the first tracer bullet.
- Develop visual direction from the work and chosen references.
- Build and review one complete project path.

### Then

Complete a bounded but real site.

- Editorial Index
- Selected Work
- Writing foundation
- About and contact
- One initial constellation, if the recovered material and schedule support it
- Responsive, accessible, performant implementation
- Deployment to `subsense.art`

### Later

- Richer constellations
- Automated or assisted capture from Recall
- Project-specific design-steering packets
- Learning paths through selected material
- Deeper relationship browsing
- Shared or guest curation
- Generalized productization

“Later” items should influence extensibility only when the cost is modest and the need is credible. They should not delay the first public site.

## 20. Explicit project boundaries

The following distinctions protect the project:

### Subsense is not Recall

Recall may feed it later. Subsense should not expose or reproduce the raw intake system.

### Subsense is not ICM

ICM may structure the build workspace. It is not the public product.

### Subsense is not Index Zero

Paths through an archive do not turn the artist site into a coaching platform.

### Subsense is not a generic taste app

The personal corpus and site come first. A generalized product can only emerge after the method proves useful in real practice.

### Subsense is not merely the old portfolio restored

Historical material anchors the site, but the structure must support Danny’s present and future practice.

### Subsense is not an explanation of an unbuilt future

Existing art, writing, and research must lead. Future capabilities should appear only when embodied in real content or behavior.

### AI is not the authorial center

Agents support recovery, structure, implementation, and critique. Danny chooses what belongs, what it means, and what appears under his name.

## 21. Questions the project should keep open

These are legitimate design questions, not blockers for beginning the inventory.

### Identity and positioning

- Is “interdisciplinary artist-researcher” still the primary public descriptor, or should the work itself carry more of that explanation?
- How visible should Danny’s architecture, fabrication, technology, and systems work be within the artist site?
- Does “Subsense” name the artist practice, the publication, the cultural atlas, or the container for all three?

### Audience

- Who must understand the site immediately: curators, collaborators, peers, commissioners, technologists, architects, general readers, or a deliberately mixed public?
- What should a first-time visitor understand within one minute?
- What should reward a thirty-minute wander?

### Content

- Which old projects remain central?
- Which have enough surviving source material for a rich reconstruction?
- What writing already exists and should become public?
- Should process evidence be visible inside project pages or in separate research notes?

### Constellations

- Is “Constellations” the right public label, or initially an internal content concept?
- Should the first constellation be “On Taste, Curation, and Authored Worlds,” or should it emerge from the art corpus itself?
- How much authored interpretation is required before a grouping earns publication?

### Visual direction

- Which qualities in the old site should be remembered even if its layout is not preserved?
- Which reference sites belong in the active design set, and what precise qualities are being borrowed from each?
- How should sound and time appear in the visual and navigational language?
- How can the site feel accumulated and inhabited at launch without simulating false history?

### Technology

- What is the simplest content system that Danny can maintain comfortably?
- Which media formats must be supported from the start?
- How should large images, audio, video, and 3D or interactive artifacts be stored and delivered?
- What parts of the site genuinely benefit from structured relationships versus ordinary editorial linking?

These questions should be answered progressively through corpus review and tracer builds, not through speculative architecture alone.

## 22. A working theory of the future product

The generalized product-shaped idea is a system that helps a person:

1. Capture references from many sources.
2. Resolve original source and provenance.
3. Identify the precise element that attracted attention.
4. Explain why it works.
5. Compare it with similar and contrasting examples.
6. Recognize recurring preferences.
7. Convert preferences into provisional principles.
8. Test those principles through making.
9. Build learning paths around weak or emerging areas.
10. Generate project-specific creative direction.
11. Compare rendered outcomes with intended effects.
12. Maintain an inspectable, editable record of changing judgment.

Possible users include artists, writers, creative directors, founders, designers, and teams developing a shared visual language.

But this is a hypothesis, not the immediate product commitment.

The correct sequence is:

```text
Make the personal system useful
→ use it to build Subsense
→ observe the recurring method
→ formalize only what proves necessary
→ test whether it generalizes
```

The website is therefore not a throwaway precursor. It is the first serious use case and evidence-generating environment.

## 23. Success criteria for the first release

The first release succeeds if:

- It truthfully represents Danny’s practice using real recovered material.
- A visitor can understand that Subsense is more than a portfolio grid without reading a manifesto about future software.
- The site supports art, writing, and at least the structural possibility of constellations.

- Danny can direct continued development without needing to become a conventional programmer.

The deeper test is:

> Does Subsense feel like the beginning of a place Danny can inhabit and develop for years?

## 25. Final orientation

The project began as a desire to rebuild a personal website. It expanded because the website touched a deeper question:

> How can accumulated attention become an authored, explorable, and usable body of judgment?

The Selvedge Yard demonstrated the soul of an inhabited cultural archive. Anam-Cara demonstrated the accessibility of opinionated pathways. Learning graphs showed that understanding travels through relationships. Recall clarified the need for durable capture and provenance. ICM clarified how the right context can be assembled for an agent. Design steering clarified how articulated taste can affect what gets made. Index Zero showed one possible future in structured capability development. Danny’s artist-researcher practice supplies the actual substance: sound, memory, land, material, computation, fabrication, narrative, and sensory experience.

Subsense is where these ideas first become public and concrete.

The immediate obligation is modest but serious: recover the work, understand it, make deliberate editorial decisions, build one complete path, and publish a site that feels inhabited by the person whose name and work it carries.

The larger possibility remains visible:

```text
Saved material
→ resolved provenance
→ interpreted relationships
→ articulated judgment
→ curated constellation
→ learning and creative direction
→ new work
→ public expression
```

The rebuild should keep that horizon without attempting to construct the entire horizon at once.
